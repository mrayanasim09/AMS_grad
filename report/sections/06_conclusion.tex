\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented a complete, reproducible investigation of \textsc{Adam}'s non-convergence in the Online Convex Optimization (OCO) setting and mapped its $(\alpha, \beta_2, C)$ phase structure.

\paragraph{Key Findings.}
\textsc{Adam}'s divergence is caused by $v_t$ decaying between gradient bursts, invalidating the OCO telescoping bound ($\Gamma_t \not\succeq 0$). Full $\bar{x}$ vs.\ $k$ profiles reveal a non-monotone, multi-island survival structure: at $C=10$ (small burst), survival spans nearly all $\beta_2$; at intermediate $C \in [30,70]$, narrow Regime II islands persist at high $\beta_2$; all islands close empirically at $C \ge 75$. The non-monotone $k^*(C)$ sequence refutes the a priori power-law scaling hypothesis. We derive the traverse threshold $\alpha^*(C) \approx 2/\sqrt{C}$, validated against bisection to within 6\% (relative to the empirical bisection value). The sub-threshold pinned-band formula has 3\% error at $\alpha=0.10$ and 17\% at $\alpha=0.20$ (systematic overestimate as the $+1$-anchoring assumption weakens). The closure of the survival island at $C \ge 75$ is an empirical finding; the upper edge of the island lacks a closed-form derivation.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
This study is restricted to $d=1$, $\mathcal{F}=[-1,1]$, linear losses $f_t(x) = g_t x$, fixed $\alpha=0.80$, $\beta_1=0.9$. The stochastic sweep uses 9 $k$-values per $C$ (sparse at low $\beta_2$) and a short horizon $T=50(C+1)$. The Fokker-Planck diffusion approximation holds at $\delta=0.10$ but underestimates concentration by $3\times$ at $\delta=0.50$. Extensions to higher dimensions, non-linear losses, and practical deep learning settings are left to future work.

\paragraph{Future Directions.}
The phase diagram suggests that a burst-aware adaptive $\beta_2$ schedule (responsive to burst detection) could narrow the survival window's dependence on $C$ without \textsc{AMSGrad}'s global-maximum conservatism. The unexplained mechanism behind the $C=10$ broad survival regime and the island upper-edge dynamics at intermediate $C$ are natural open questions.
