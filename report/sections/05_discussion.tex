\section{Discussion, Practical Implications \& Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Two-Regime Summary}
Our mechanism discrimination experiments reveal two structurally distinct divergence regimes at large gradient burst spacing:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Regime I (v-Collapse, $\tau_2 \lesssim T_{\text{burst}}$):} The second-moment estimate decays between bursts, amplifying the effective learning rate on non-burst steps. This regime fails unconditionally for all learning rates $\alpha$ tested (Probe P2, $\beta_2 = 0.9$ column, $C=100$). The fix (\textsc{AMSGrad}) is precisely targeted at this regime.
    \item \textbf{Regime II (Post-Burst Drift, $\tau_2 \gg T_{\text{burst}}$):} With high $\beta_2$, $v_t$ remains approximately constant. Failure is governed by the traverse condition $\alpha(C - s^*(C)) \gtrsim 2\sqrt{C}$, where $s^*(C) = \tau_1 \ln((1-\beta_1)C+1)$ is the protected window during which the first moment $\tilde{m}_t$ remains positive after a burst, and $\tau_1 = 1/(1-\beta_1)$ is the first-moment memory horizon. At $C=100$, the predicted threshold $\alpha^*=0.263$ matches the empirically bisected boundary $\alpha^*=0.251$ to within $5\%$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Practical Conservatism of AMSGrad}
\textsc{AMSGrad} successfully resolves Regime I by enforcing $\hat{v}_t \ge \hat{v}_{t-1}$. This guarantees $\Gamma_t \succeq 0$ and restores sublinear regret $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{T})$ under decreasing step sizes. However, in non-stationary deep learning settings, the historical maximum $\hat{v}_t$ can be locked by a single early outlier gradient burst, depressing future coordinate-wise learning rates and inducing practical sluggishness.

\subsection{Conclusion}
In this work, we presented a complete, reproducible replication of the \textsc{Adam} $\to$ \textsc{AMSGrad} transition and a pre-registered parametric extension. Our primary findings are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Exact Counterexample Reproduction:} Both deterministic and stochastic Reddi et al.\ counterexamples are reproduced, with live $\Gamma_t$ tracking visually confirming the OCO semi-definiteness breakdown.
    \item \textbf{Boundary Disappearance:} Under rigorous cycle-averaged metrics, the divergence boundary exists only at $C=30$ ($k^* = 0.300 \pm 5 \times 10^{-5}$, $\beta_2^* = 0.990 \pm 2 \times 10^{-6}$; bisection half-intervals). For $C \ge 100$, \textsc{Adam} fails unconditionally across all $\beta_2 \in [0.05,\, 1-10^{-6}]$, refuting the pre-registered $C^{-1}$ scaling law. At $C=10$, behavior is mixed: survival at high $\beta_2$ and failure at $\beta_2=0.5$.
    \item \textbf{Two-Regime Mechanism:} v-collapse drives failure at low $\tau_2$; post-burst first-moment drift governs failure at large $\tau_2$. The traverse threshold $\alpha^*(C) = 2\sqrt{C}/(C - s^*(C))$ is quantitatively validated at $C=100$ (5\% error) and $C=300$ (6\% error).
\end{enumerate}
